FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What you might want to know about AlgoSoft and its products.

What is AlgoSoft?

AlgoSoft is a developer-tools brand with six products: AlgoCOM, AlgoTerm, AlgoGit, AlgoPDF, AlgoCode and AlgoClass.

What do AlgoCOM, AlgoTerm, AlgoGit and AlgoPDF do?

AlgoCOM is a serial communication and debugging tool, AlgoTerm is a terminal emulator, AlgoGit is a visual Git client, and AlgoPDF is a PDF toolbox.

What is the relationship between AlgoCode and AlgoClass?

They are an integrated solution for teaching C++ algorithms: AlgoCode is the student IDE and AlgoClass is the teacher's classroom tool, used together.

Are these products free?

AlgoCode and AlgoClass are free to use. AlgoCOM, AlgoTerm, AlgoGit and AlgoPDF are not released yet; pricing will be announced later.

Where can I download the software?

AlgoCode and AlgoClass can be downloaded from algocode.cc. AlgoCOM, AlgoTerm, AlgoGit and AlgoPDF are not released yet.

What is the relationship between algosoft.cc and algocode.cc?

algosoft.cc is the official site of the AlgoSoft product family; algocode.cc is the online site of AlgoCode and AlgoClass.
